## 2. Customized Learning Methodology (The 3-4 Hour Daily Constraint)

Balancing school (Class 11-12) with your extreme multidisciplinary ambitions requires ruthless efficiency.

### A. The "Anti-Tutorial Hell" Protocol
- **Stop Watching, Start Building:** Never watch a tutorial without immediately coding a broken version of it yourself.
- **Project-Driven Learning:** Learn PyTorch/JAX by building a simple neural network that predicts protein sequences, not by following a generic dog/cat classifier tutorial.

### B. Daily Schedule Breakdown (3-4 Hours)
*Rotate these blocks throughout the week to maintain momentum across all fields.*

**Block 1: Computational Foundations (1.5 Hours - 3x/week)**
- **Focus:** Hybrid classical-quantum computing (PyTorch/JAX).
- **Action:** Transition from basic Python to JAX arrays. JAX's auto-differentiation is crucial for both ML and quantum simulations.

**Block 2: Algorithmic Trading as Data Science Practice (1.5 Hours - 2x/week)**
- **Focus:** Applied Machine Learning & Statistical Modeling.
- **Action:** Use Algorithmic Trading as your testing ground for time-series data, risk management, and ML. Financial data is noisy and complex, exactly like biological data. If you can model market microstructure, you can model gene expression.

**Block 3: Bioinformatics & Genetic Engineering (1.5 Hours - 3x/week)**
- **Focus:** Foundational RNA-Seq / scRNA-Seq analysis.
- **Action:** Single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-Seq) is the fundamental currency of modern biology. Learn to process FASTQ files to count matrices using tools like Seurat or Scanpy.

**Block 4: Quantum Mechanics & Biology Integration (1.5 Hours - 2x/week)**
- **Focus:** Physics fundamentals & Quantum computing logic.
- **Action:** Study linear algebra and quantum gates (Qiskit). Read literature on UChicago's quantum biological sensors to see the end-goal.

## 3. Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **The "Too Broad" Trap:** You want to do everything at once. You must find the intersections. (e.g., using Quantum ML to optimize Algorithmic Trading models, or using ML to analyze scRNA-Seq data).
2. **Ignoring Mathematics:** Quantum mechanics and ML are just applied linear algebra and probability. Do not skip the math foundations in Class 11-12.
3. **Burnout:** Biological immortality is a marathon. Working 12 hours a day and burning out in a month is counterproductive. Stick rigidly to your 3-4 hour limit.
