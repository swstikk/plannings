## ⚠️ 3. COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID (Course Corrections)

As a 16-year-old polymath, these are the traps that will slow you down:

### ❌ Mistake 1: "Tutorial Hell"

* **The Trap**: Watching 50 hours of YouTube videos on Python, PyTorch, or Qiskit without building anything.
* **The Fix**: Adopt **Project-Based Learning**. You learn PyTorch *by* trying to build a basic sequence classifier for DNA. You learn Qiskit *by* trying to build the H2 molecule simulation. If you don't know how to do a step, Google it. This is how real engineers work.

### ❌ Mistake 2: Skipping Foundational Math for Quantum/ML

* **The Trap**: Jumping straight into running VQE code without understanding what a Hamiltonian or a Unitary Matrix is. (Your `quantum_bio_master_blueprint.md` actually addresses this perfectly, but you must *stick to it*).
* **The Fix**: Linear Algebra and Calculus are your gods now. If you don't understand eigenvalues and eigenvectors, AlphaFold and Quantum Computing will look like black magic. Spend the time on the math.

### ❌ Mistake 3: Treating Memory as a Task, Not Context

* **The Trap**: Memorizing pathways (like the Krebs cycle) instead of understanding the system.
* **The Fix**: Biology is becoming an engineering discipline. Don't memorize; understand the *logic* of the system. Why does the cell do this? How can we engineer a protein to change it?

### ❌ Mistake 4: Burning Out on "Too Many Paths"

* **The Trap**: Trying to do Algo Trading, Genetic Engineering, and Quantum Physics all on Monday.
* **The Fix**: Use **Sprints**. Spend 3 weeks doing a heavy sprint on Machine Learning and Algo Trading. Then spend 3 weeks doing a heavy sprint on Molecular Bio and Bioinformatics. The context switching of doing 4 diverse fields every single day will destroy your focus.
