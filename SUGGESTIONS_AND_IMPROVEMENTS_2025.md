- **The Fix:** For every 1 hour of video, you must spend 2 hours building something without looking at the tutorial. (e.g., Code a simple Moving Average trading bot, or solve a Rosalind bioinformatics problem).

### 🧮 Trap 2: Skipping the Math

- **The Mistake:** Jumping straight into Quantum Computing code (Qiskit) or Deep Learning without understanding Linear Algebra.
- **The Fix:** The roadmap emphasizes this, but I am reinforcing it. **Linear Algebra is the absolute foundation of both Quantum Computing and Machine Learning.** Do not skip the math modules.

- **The Mistake:** Believing that because you can simulate a protein in Python, you understand biology. Biology is messy.
- **The Fix:** Keep grounded in the chemical realities (Tier 1.4 Biochemistry). Understand *how* the data you are computing was generated (e.g., how RNA-Seq actually works physically).

- **The Mistake:** Trying to finish Ph.D. level material before graduating high school.
- **The Fix:** Treat "Project Prometheus" as a marathon. Your primary goal right now is **Foundations**. Master Python, Linear Algebra, basic Machine Learning, and Central Dogma Biology. You have the rest of your life for the advanced research.
