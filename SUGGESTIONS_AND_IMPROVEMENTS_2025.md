* **Stem Cell Organoids & Rejuvenation (2025 Breakthroughs):** iPSCs are now successfully creating complex organoids for tissue replacement. New microencapsulation delivery systems significantly improve stem cell retention in aging tissues.
* **CRISPR & Stem Cell Fusion:** The biggest trend in 2025 is using CRISPR-Cas9 to edit genes *within* stem cells to enhance their longevity properties—cranking up cellular repair genes and downregulating degenerative genes.
* **In-Market Therapeutics:** Keep a close eye on the long-term data of CRISPR therapies like **Casgevy** (now expanding beyond initial indications) and the mainstreaming of **GLP-1 agonists** which are showing epigenetic aging slowdown and multi-system rejuvenation beyond weight loss.

* **Biological AI Models:** Major 2024/2025 models like **AlphaFold3**, **OpenFold3**, and **Boltz-1** are bridging the gap between computer science and biology. These tools are non-negotiable for modern structural biologists and drug discoverers.

### C. Quantum Biology & Machine Learning

* **Hybrid AI-Quantum Drug Discovery:** Paradigms like **Majorana-1** and **GALILEO** represent the cutting edge of hybrid classical-quantum models to simulate molecular docking for drug discovery.
