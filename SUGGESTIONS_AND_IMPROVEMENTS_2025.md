## 🔬 1. 2025 REAL-TIME BREAKTHROUGHS TO INTEGRATE

### A. Aging & Longevity (The Core Goal)

1. **GLP-1 as a True Longevity Drug:** GLP-1 drugs are no longer just for weight loss. They are showing multi-system rejuvenation, reducing inflammation and acting as broad-spectrum metabolic health protectors.
2. **SGLT2 Inhibitors LENGTHENING Telomeres:** A massive 2025 human study showed SGLT2 inhibitors *actually increasing* telomere length and reducing cellular senescence. This is arguably the first proven intervention in humans.
3. **Rapamycin Combinations:** The Interventions Testing Program (ITP) synthesized two decades of work, showing that combination therapies (like Rapamycin + Acarbose) outperform single interventions significantly (up to 36% median lifespan increase in mice).

### B. Quantum Biology & Bioinformatics

1. **Biological Qubits (UChicago Breakthrough):** Scientists successfully turned a protein found in living cells into a biological quantum bit (qubit). This allows quantum sensors to "see" inside living cells with extreme precision. *This is exactly the intersection of your Quantum Computing and Biology interests.*
2. **AlphaFold3 & Boltz-1:** The transition from predicting just proteins to predicting all of life's biomolecules (DNA, RNA, small molecule ligands) is now open-source and standard.
3. **RNA Knowledge Graphs (RNA-KG):** Bioinformatics advanced with homogeneous embedding methods for discovering novel non-coding RNA interactions.

## ⚠️ 2. COMMON MISTAKES FOR YOUR PROFILE (And How to Avoid Them)

You are attempting to master **five** of the hardest fields in human history simultaneously at age 16, within 3-4 hours a day.

* **Mistake 1: The "Tutorial Hell" Trap**
  * *Symptom:* Watching YouTube videos of Python/Qiskit or Biology without writing code or recalling information.
  * *Fix:* **Implement to learn.** If you learn about a Hamiltonian, immediately code it in NumPy. If you learn about DNA translation, write a Python script to translate a DNA sequence to amino acids.
* **Mistake 2: Context Switching Penalty**
  * *Symptom:* Doing 20 mins of Bio, 20 mins of QC, 20 mins of ML, 20 mins of Trading. Your brain never reaches "deep work".
  * *Fix:* **Interleaving by Days, not Minutes.** E.g., Monday = Math/QC + Algorithmic Trading, Tuesday = ML + Bioinformatics.
* **Mistake 3: Treating Algorithmic Trading as a Distraction**
  * *Symptom:* Viewing trading as separate from your science goals.
  * *Fix:* **Merge Trading and ML.** Use algorithmic trading as your *practice ground* for Time-Series Analysis, Reinforcement Learning, and Statistical Modeling. The ML skills you build predicting stock movements are mathematically identical to predicting biological sequence patterns.
* **Mistake 4: Skipping the Math**
  * *Symptom:* Jumping straight to building neural networks or quantum circuits without understanding Linear Algebra and Eigenvalues.
  * *Fix:* Stick rigidly to the first 9 weeks of your *Project Prometheus* blueprint. Quantum Mechanics is just Linear Algebra in complex space.
