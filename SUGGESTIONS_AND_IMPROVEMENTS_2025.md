## ⚠️ 3. COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

1. **The "Silo" Trap**: Don't treat QC, ML, and Bio as separate subjects. They are the *same* subject. Use ML to analyze Bio data; use QC to simulate the Bio data that ML couldn't process.
2. **Ignoring Foundational Math**: Rushing to implement Variational Quantum Eigensolvers (VQE) without deeply understanding Eigenvalues, Hilbert spaces, and Tensor Products will block your progress later. Your math base *must* be unbreakable.
3. **Over-Optimizing Tools**: Don't spend weeks debating PyTorch vs. JAX or Qiskit vs. PennyLane. Pick the industry standard (PyTorch + Qiskit for now) and master the underlying concepts, not the syntax.
4. **Neglecting Rest**: Biological immortality research requires your brain to function optimally. 3-4 hours of intense, focused deep-work is better than 8 hours of distracted studying.
