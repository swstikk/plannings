### A. AI in Biology & Protein Design

* **AlphaFold 3 & OpenFold3:** DeepMind's AlphaFold 3 is now capable of predicting structures not just for proteins, but for all of life's molecules (DNA, RNA, ligands, ions) and their interactions. OpenFold3 provides open-source alternatives.
  * *How this affects you:* Your ML roadmap MUST prioritize Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) and Transformers. Do not get stuck just learning basic CNNs for image classification. Molecular data is graph-based and sequence-based.

### B. Longevity & Genetic Engineering

* **CRISPR Casgevy:** The first CRISPR therapy (Casgevy) was FDA approved for sickle cell disease. This is proof that in-vivo gene editing is now a commercial reality, not just lab science.
* **Senolytics & PEARL Trial:** The PEARL (Participatory Evaluation of Aging with Rapamycin for Longevity) trial is yielding critical data on low-dose Rapamycin. Senolytics (drugs that clear zombie cells) and GLP-1 agonists are showing multi-system rejuvenation effects.
* **Programmable Chromosomal Engineering:** Moving beyond single gene edits, scientists are now writing large chunks of synthetic chromosomes.

### C. Quantum Computing & Hybrid Systems

* **Practical Quantum Simulation:** IBM and others are pushing Qiskit towards utility scale. However, pure quantum algorithms are still noisy.
* **Hybrid Classical-Quantum (HCQ):** The real world in 2025 uses PyTorch/JAX classical neural networks combined with Quantum Variational circuits (VQE). *Do not study quantum mechanics in isolation; study it alongside PyTorch.*
