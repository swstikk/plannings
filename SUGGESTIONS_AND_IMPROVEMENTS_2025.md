- **AlphaFold 3**: DeepMind's AlphaFold 3 (released May 2024) significantly improved the prediction of all life's molecules, not just proteins (DNA, RNA, small molecules).

- **Actionable Insight**: Prioritize exploring AlphaFold 3 and Boltz-1 over older models. You can now predict protein-ligand interactions much more accurately, which is vital for your Quantum-Bio goals.

- **Actionable Insight**: In your Tier 1B Genetic Engineering syllabus, add a focus on miniaturized Cas enzymes and in-vivo delivery methods (AAV/LNPs), as this is the new frontier.
