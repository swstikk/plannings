## ⚠️ 3. COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

1. **Skipping Foundational Mathematics**
   - **The Trap**: Wanting to code quantum algorithms before understanding eigenvectors.
   - **The Fix**: Master Linear Algebra (matrices, eigenvalues, complex numbers) and Calculus. You cannot do Quantum Computing or Deep Learning without them.

2. **Context Switching Too Rapidly**
   - **The Trap**: Spending 20 minutes on genetic engineering, 20 minutes on trading, and 20 minutes on quantum physics.
   - **The Fix**: Dedicate specific days to specific integrations. (e.g., Monday: Quantum Math, Tuesday: ML Trading).

3. **Ignoring Class 11-12 Academics**
   - **The Trap**: Thinking school is irrelevant to "real" science.
   - **The Fix**: High school physics (mechanics, waves) and chemistry (thermodynamics, atomic structure) are the *exact* prerequisites for Quantum Biology. Use your school time as foundational training.

4. **Getting Stuck in "Tutorial Hell"**
   - **The Trap**: Doing endless Python courses without building anything.
   - **The Fix**: Learn by doing. If you want to learn JAX, try implementing a very basic Neural Network yourself instead of following a 10-hour course.
