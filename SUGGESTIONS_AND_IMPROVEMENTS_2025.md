## 🧬 REAL-TIME 2025 INDUSTRY BREAKTHROUGHS
Your roadmap is excellent, but you need to align with what happened *this year*.

### Structural Biology & AI
* **AlphaFold3 & OpenFold3**: Structural biology is entirely solved for static single-chain proteins. The 2025 meta is predicting **protein-ligand complexes**, **RNA structures**, and **conformational dynamics**. Focus your ML efforts on *dynamic* binding, not static folding.
* **Boltz-1**: Biomolecular interaction prediction is the new frontier. It goes beyond proteins to predict how they interact with DNA/RNA and small molecules.

### Quantum Biology & Sensors
* **UChicago Quantum Biological Sensors**: The real "Quantum Biology" isn't just theory anymore. Researchers are using nanodiamonds (NV centers) to measure magnetic and thermal gradients *inside living cells*. This is the bridge between physics and biology.
* **Majorana-1 & GALILEO**: These are the early 2025 hybrid classical-quantum drug discovery pipelines. They use classical GPUs (PyTorch/JAX) for 99% of the work, and Quantum Processing Units (QPUs) to compute exact ground-state energies (VQE) for the hardest 1% of electron interactions in the active site.

### Epigenetics & Longevity (The Immortality Goal)
* **GLP-1 Multi-System Rejuvenators**: Drugs originally for weight loss (semaglutide/tirzepatide) are showing massive systemic anti-aging effects in 2025 data. They reduce neuroinflammation, improve cardiovascular health, and seemingly slow epigenetic aging.
* **Senolytics & SGLT2 Inhibitors**: The clearance of "zombie cells" (senescence) is moving from mouse models to solid human trials. SGLT2 inhibitors (like empagliflozin) are also mimicking fasting states, increasing lifespan in mammalian models.
* **Low-Dose Rapamycin (PEARL Trial)**: The human trials for rapamycin are yielding results. The focus is now on the exact dosing schedule to inhibit mTORC1 without hitting mTORC2 (which causes immune suppression).
* **CRISPR Therapies (Casgevy)**: FDA approvals for in-vivo and ex-vivo CRISPR therapies are expanding. The engineering bottleneck is no longer the edit itself, but the **delivery system** (LNPs and tissue-specific AAVs).

Your biology roadmap is strong, but before you do complex Systems Biology, you must master **RNA-Seq and scRNA-Seq (single-cell)** data analysis.
* Why? Transcriptomics is the *ground truth* for aging. If you want to know if a drug reversed aging, you measure the RNA expression of the cells.
* **Action**: Learn the pipeline: `FASTQ -> STAR aligner -> featureCounts -> DESeq2 (R or Python)`.

* If you are learning ML in 2025 for biology and quantum, ignore older frameworks. Learn PyTorch and JAX.
* AlphaFold is built on JAX. Modern quantum ML libraries heavily integrate with JAX because of its superior autograd capabilities for physics.
