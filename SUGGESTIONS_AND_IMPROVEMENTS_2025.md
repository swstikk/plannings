## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Context Switching Too Fast:** Trying to do 1 hour of Quantum, 1 hour of Bio, 1 hour of Trading every single day will lead to burnout and shallow knowledge.
   - *Fix:* Use "Block Scheduling." Do a 2-week deep dive into one specific sub-topic (e.g., Neural Networks), then apply it to trading, then apply it to bioinformatics.
2. **Ignoring Mathematics:** Skipping the underlying math to get to the "cool code" faster.
   - *Fix:* As stated in your Prometheus blueprint, Linear Algebra and Eigenvalue theory are non-negotiable.
3. **Reinventing the Wheel:** Building everything from scratch.
   - *Fix:* Use existing state-of-the-art tools (AlphaFold3, PyTorch, Qiskit) rather than trying to write your own basic neural network from scratch in C++.
4. **Neglecting Classical Physics:** Jumping into Quantum Biology without understanding Classical Thermodynamics and Statistical Mechanics.
   - *Fix:* Understand free energy and entropy; they govern protein folding and molecular interactions.
