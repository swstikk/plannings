## 📈 HOW TO USE ALGORITHMIC TRADING TO PRACTICE DATA SCIENCE

Algo trading is the ultimate, unforgiving test of your Data Science/ML skills. The market provides instant feedback.

1. **Start with Clean Data**: Use free APIs (like Yahoo Finance via `yfinance` in Python) to pull historical daily data.
2. **Feature Engineering**: Calculate moving averages, RSI, MACD. This teaches you time-series data manipulation (Pandas).
3. **Machine Learning**: Train models (Random Forests, LSTMs) to predict price direction (up/down), NOT exact prices.
4. **Backtesting**: Write a script to test your model on past data. **Crucial**: Account for trading fees and slippage, otherwise your model will look artificially profitable.
5. **The Goal**: The goal right now isn't to get rich; it's to force yourself to build end-to-end ML pipelines.
