You are trying to learn 5 highly advanced, distinct fields in 3-4 hours a day while in Class 11/12. This is historically impossible without extreme optimization.

### The "Stacking" Strategy (Do Not Learn In Parallel)

Attempting to do 1 hour of Quantum, 1 hour of Bio, 1 hour of ML, and 1 hour of Trading every day will lead to cognitive fragmentation and burnout.

Instead, use **Skill Stacking**:

1. **Algorithmic Trading as the ML Sandbox:** Do not learn ML in a vacuum. Use financial data (Algo Trading) to learn time-series forecasting, reinforcement learning, and PyTorch. This hits two goals at once.
2. **Quantum Bioinformatics:** Learn Quantum Mechanics *through* the lens of molecular simulation (VQE algorithms for molecular ground states).

### The Daily Protocol (The 3.5 Hour Optimization)

* **Hour 1: Core Foundation (Math/Physics):** Linear Algebra, Calculus, or Quantum Mechanics. (e.g., from your `quantum_bio_master_blueprint.md`).
* **Hour 2: Code Implementation (ML/Algo Trading):** Implementing the math in Python (PyTorch/JAX).
* **Hour 3: Domain Knowledge (Genetics/Bioinformatics):** Reading about CRISPR, RNA-Seq, or studying biological pathways.
* **Final 30 Mins: Synthesis/Spaced Repetition:** Using Anki to memorize biological pathways, mathematical formulas, and coding syntax.
