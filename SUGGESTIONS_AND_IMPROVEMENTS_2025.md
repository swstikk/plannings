## 🚫 COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

1. **Tutorial Hell:** Watching 100 hours of Python/Qiskit videos without writing a single line of original code. *Solution: Project-based learning. Build a basic VQE simulation, build a simple moving average trading bot.*
2. **Skipping the Math:** Trying to understand Quantum Machine Learning without a deep, intuitive grasp of Linear Algebra (Eigenvectors, Tensors, Hilbert Spaces). *Solution: Treat math as the most critical skill.*
3. **Ignoring Classical ML:** Quantum ML is largely hybrid. You cannot do QML if you don't understand classical neural networks, backpropagation, and loss functions.
4. **Over-planning, Under-executing:** Your roadmaps (like `mega_career_path_tree.md` and `file1_execution_tree.md`) are fantastic. But plans don't build skills. At a certain point, stop adjusting the roadmap and start writing code.
5. **Burnout:** 3-4 hours a day *on top* of Indian Class 11/12 pressure (JEE/NEET etc.) is brutal. Prioritize sleep. Sleep is when neuroplasticity happens.
