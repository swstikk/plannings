## ⚠️ 2. COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID (The "Tutorial Hell" Trap)

Given your incredibly ambitious parallel tracks (Quantum, ML, Bio, Algo Trading), you are at high risk for specific failure modes.

1. **"Tutorial Hell" in Coding**:
    * *Mistake*: Watching endless Python, Qiskit, or ML tutorials without building anything.
    * *Fix*: Practice "Implementation-First." If you learn about a quantum gate, code it immediately. If you learn about a biological concept (e.g., GC content), write a Python script to calculate it that same day.
2. **The Context-Switching Tax**:
    * *Mistake*: Trying to do Quantum, Bio, ML, and Trading *all in the same 3-4 hour block*. Your brain will not retain the information.
    * *Fix*: Use the block schedule you defined. Dedicate specific days to specific domains, or use strict 90-minute boundaries. Never switch domains more than twice a day.
3. **Ignoring the Math Foundation**:
    * *Mistake*: Jumping straight into Qiskit or PyTorch without understanding Linear Algebra and Calculus.
    * *Fix*: Stick to your "Module M1-M4" plan. Do not skip the math. You cannot understand VQE or backpropagation without a rock-solid understanding of matrices and eigenvalues.
4. **Neglecting School Grades (Class 11/12)**:
    * *Mistake*: Focusing so much on your PhD-level roadmap that your Class 12 board exams or entrance exams suffer.
    * *Fix*: Map your school syllabus to your roadmap. When studying Class 12 Genetics, use it as the foundation for Tier 1 Biology. Ace your standard curriculum as the baseline.
