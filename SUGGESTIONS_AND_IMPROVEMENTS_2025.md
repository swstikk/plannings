## ⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

1. **Context Switching Penalty**: Trying to do 5 things in a single 3-hour session. **Fix**: Dedicate specific days or deep 90-minute blocks to a single domain, but ensure the *project* connects the domains.
2. **Ignoring Math Foundations**: Both Quantum Computing and ML rely heavily on Linear Algebra and Calculus. Do not skip the math to get to the coding. (Reference: *Project Prometheus Dimension 1.1*).
3. **Tutorial Illusion of Competence**: Believing you understand an algorithm because you watched a video on it. **Fix**: Write the algorithm from scratch in NumPy or PyTorch without looking at the tutorial.
4. **Neglecting Classical Biology**: You cannot engineer what you do not understand. Ensure your grasp of molecular biology (Tier 1 & Tier 2 in your roadmap) is rock solid before attempting synthetic biology or programmable chromosomal engineering.
