* **The News:** Recent NIH and AHA publications have revealed that **SGLT2 inhibitors** (originally anti-diabetic drugs) have potent **senolytic effects**. * **Why it matters for you:** Your ultimate goal is biological immortality. This represents a massive shift from complex gene therapies to repurposing existing small molecules for anti-aging.

### C. Gene Editing: Miniaturization of CRISPR (NIH Al3Cas12f)

* **The News:** An NIH-funded team engineered **Al3Cas12f**, an ultra-compact CRISPR enzyme small enough to fit into a single Adeno-Associated Virus (AAV) vector, achieving 80%+ in vivo editing efficiency.
* **Why it matters for you:** The biggest hurdle in genetic engineering was delivery. Al3Cas12f solves the payload size problem.
* **Actionable Step:** Focus your molecular biology studies not just on the editing mechanism (how Cas9 works) but heavily on **viral vector delivery systems** and compact nucleases.

### D. Quantum Computing: Hybrid Architectures
