## ⚠️ 3. COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

As a 16-year-old polymath-in-training, you are at high risk for the following traps:

1. **The "Tutorial Hell" Trap:**
   - *Mistake:* Watching 50 hours of YouTube videos on PyTorch and thinking you know PyTorch.
   - *Fix:* For every 1 hour of video, you must spend 2 hours writing code that breaks, debugging it, and fixing it. Build actual projects.

2. **The "Context-Switching" Tax:**
   - *Mistake:* Studying biology for 20 minutes, then quantum mechanics for 20 minutes, then trading for 20 minutes.
   - *Fix:* Deep work. With 3-4 hours a day, allocate **entire days** or **90-minute blocks** to a single subject. (e.g., Monday: Math/Quantum, Tuesday: Bio/Genetics, Wednesday: ML/Algo). Your brain needs time to load the context.

3. **Ignoring the Math Foundation:**
   - *Mistake:* Jumping straight into Qiskit or deep learning libraries without understanding Linear Algebra and Calculus.
   - *Fix:* The algorithms will look like magic, and when they fail, you won't know why. Your plan to study Linear Algebra (Module M1-M4) is critical. Do not skip it.

4. **Burnout Before Class 12 Ends:**
   - *Mistake:* Trying to operate at 100% capacity for 2 years straight without breaks.
   - *Fix:* Your physical health (sleep, exercise, diet) is step zero of biological immortality. A sleep-deprived brain cannot learn quantum mechanics. Prioritize 8 hours of sleep over an extra hour of studying.
