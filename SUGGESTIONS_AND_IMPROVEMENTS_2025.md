## ⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

1. **Context-Switching Burnout**: Trying to learn Quantum Physics, Cell Culturing, and Algorithmic Trading on the *same day*.
   * **Fix**: Use "Theme Days" or "Theme Weeks." E.g., Week 1: Bioinformatics + ML. Week 2: Quantum Math + Algo Trading.
2. **Skipping Foundational Math**: You cannot do Quantum Computing or Advanced ML without Linear Algebra, Calculus, and Probability.
   * **Fix**: Treat Mathematics as the absolute non-negotiable core. A weak math foundation will crash your entire roadmap in Year 2.
3. **Optimizing for Tools over Concepts**: Learning specific UI tools (like a specific bioinformatics website) instead of learning the underlying algorithm (like how sequence alignment math actually works). Tools change; math doesn't.
4. **Ignoring Sleep**: You are 16. Neuroplasticity requires sleep. Cutting sleep to hit your 4-hour quota will destroy memory consolidation.
