## ⚠️ 2. COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

1. **"Tutorial Hell":** Spending 100 hours watching Khan Academy or Qiskit tutorials without writing a single line of code or analyzing real data.
   * *Fix:* For every 1 hour of video, you must spend 1 hour building. Download a real RNA-seq dataset from GEO or write a trading backtest.
2. **Context Switching Burnout:** Trying to do Bio, ML, Quantum, and Trading *every single day* will destroy your focus within a week.
   * *Fix:* Use **Theme Days** or **Weekly Sprints**. Example: Mon/Wed for Quantum-Bio, Tue/Thu for Algorithmic Trading/ML, Fri for Bioinformatics implementation.
3. **Ignoring the Math Foundation:** Linear Algebra and Calculus are the languages of Quantum Mechanics, ML, and Bioinformatics.
   * *Fix:* Your blueprint correctly allocates 9 weeks to Math. Do not rush this. If you don't understand eigenvectors, you won't understand quantum states or principal component analysis (PCA) in scRNA-seq.
4. **Neglecting Rest:** At 16, your brain is physically remodeling. Sleep is when neural pathways (learning) actually solidify.
   * *Fix:* 3-4 hours of deep, focused work is superior to 8 hours of distracted grinding. Guard your sleep fiercely.
